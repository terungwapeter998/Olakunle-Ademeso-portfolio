Executive Profile

Built on the operations floor, now leading from the top.

Olakunle Samuel Ademeso is a results-driven supply chain and logistics professional with extensive, hands-on experience across warehouse operations, inventory management, transportation coordination and procurement support. His work spans FMCG and industrial environments, where he has consistently focused on tightening operational efficiency, protecting inventory accuracy, and strengthening supplier and customer relationships.

His approach pairs disciplined, data-informed decision-making with direct operational leadership supervising teams, negotiating transport rates, and resolving the day-to-day friction that determines whether a supply chain runs on time or falls behind. That grounding in execution now shapes his direction as Founder & CEO of Mesokurtic Supply Chain Ltd, where he is building technology-driven infrastructure for the logistics operations he has spent his career inside of.
